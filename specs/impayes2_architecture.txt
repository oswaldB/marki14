COMPONENT COMMUNICATION:
------------------------
All components communicate through the central Alpine.js store:
- $store.impayesStore.currentView → Current view
- $store.impayesStore.getGroupedData() → Grouped data for current view
- $store.impayesStore.getGroupDisplayInfo() → Display info for groups
- $store.impayesStore.toggleGroupExpansion() → Expand/collapse groups
- $store.impayesStore.openDetailsDrawer() → Open details panel
